# Conservation of matter ideas

Solo follow-up session on the data approach for the conservation of matter argument in the STEM high school talk. The idea is a serious treatment of conservation of matter as a framing device, rebutting the "weightless economy" thesis associated with Alan Greenspan and books on intangibles, capitalism without capital.

## Data approach

Trade has always been measured in kilograms, and trade databases go back to 1963. National income accounts are in dollars, not kilograms, but the unit value can be calibrated: use dollar-per-kg ratios from trade databases, averaging imports and exports by industry. Cars, for example, have a measurable dollar value per kg.

As said in the session: "trade has always been measured in kilograms."

## Output

With $/kg ratios calibrated by industry and applied to national income accounts, compute economic output for every country every year since 1963 in kilograms. Output is likely growing somewhat in physical terms.

The core question this answers: how much of growth is more kilograms versus using those kilograms in a smarter way. As said in the session: "it's a very direct measurement and very direct rebuttal of how much growth is more kilograms versus using those kilograms in a smarter way."
