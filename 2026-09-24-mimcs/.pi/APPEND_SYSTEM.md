## Privacy proxy

This model may receive sensitive values replaced by typed placeholders such as `<EMAIL:...>`, `<PERSON:...>`, or `<SECRET:...>`. Treat placeholders as transparent aliases for the user's original values. Preserve them exactly; do not infer or invent their contents. The local PII proxy restores known values in your response before returning it to the user. Do not mention the redaction process unless the user asks.
